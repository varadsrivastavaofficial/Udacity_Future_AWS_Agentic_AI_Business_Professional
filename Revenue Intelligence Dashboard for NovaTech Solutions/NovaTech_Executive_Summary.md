# NovaTech Revenue Intelligence — Executive Summary

## Sales Pipeline

The sheet provides a comprehensive overview of sales pipeline performance, analysing deal values, loss reasons, closing timelines, and product performance across 499 opportunities totaling $707,201 in deal value.

- NovaPulse Professional dominates with the highest total deal value of $208,724, while NovaEdge Lite trails significantly at just $3,251.
- Enterprise companies generate the highest average deal value at $1,589.17, outperforming Medium-sized companies at $1,353.30.
- NovaPulse Ultimate takes the longest to close at 99.5 days, nearly double NovaPulse Professional's 60.42 days.
- Budget Constraints is the least common loss reason with only 31 occurrences, followed by Timing Not Right at 33.

## Customer Health

The sheet provides an overview of support ticket activity across companies, resolution efficiency, product areas, and customer sentiment for a dataset of 3,000 tickets.

- YieldMax Software dominates with 334 support tickets, nearly double the next highest company, TrueNorth Electronics at 180.
- Resolution times are remarkably uniform across priorities—low (1.97 days), high (1.92 days), and medium (1.92 days)—suggesting priority level has minimal impact on resolution speed.
- Notifications is the most ticket-heavy product area with 601 tickets, while Data Pipeline has the fewest at 310.
- Customer sentiment skews neutral (1,953 tickets), with negative sentiments (684) outnumbering positive ones (304) by more than two to one.

## Marketing Funnel

The sheet analyses marketing campaign performance across channels, covering response rates, lead distribution, ROI, revenue attribution, and spend efficiency.

- Direct Mail leads in campaign response rate at 53.02%, but all channels show negative ROI, with Direct Mail performing best at -70.74%.
- Partner Referral dominates revenue attribution at $676,976.95 out of a total $1,127,223.09, while also generating the most leads (807 of 2,240).
- Total campaign spend is $12,359,497.
- NovaPulse Launch is the top revenue-generating campaign at $394,156.59, while Digital Retarget has the highest spend at $2,423,781.95.
